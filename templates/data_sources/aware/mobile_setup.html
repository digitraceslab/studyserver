Step 1: Download and install AWARE

  1. Download AWARE on your phone.
    Download AWARE The file is called aware-phone-release.apk
  2. Open Downloads in your browser’s menu and tap the downloaded file.
  3. AWARE is downloaded from outside the Google Play Store, so Play Protect may warn you about it. AWARE is safe to install: tap More details, then Install anyway. If there’s no Install anyway option, pause Play Protect first.

If AWARE is blocked with no Install anyway option

On newer Android versions, Play Protect may show App blocked to protect your device with only a Got it button. Pause Play Protect’s app scanning, then install AWARE again. While paused, Play Protect doesn’t scan apps from outside Google Play, and scanning turns back on by itself the next day.

  1. Tap Got it.
  2. Open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile picture at the top right, then tap Play Protect.
  3. Tap the settings gear at the top right.
  4. Turn off Scan apps with Play Protect, then tap Pause. Choose Pause rather than Turn off, so scanning comes back on by itself.
  5. Go back to Downloads, open the AWARE file again and install it.

If your browser blocks the install

In your phone’s Settings, search for Install unknown apps. Select your browser (for example, Chrome), turn on Allow from this source, then open the downloaded file again.

Step 3: Turn on accessibility access

AWARE needs accessibility access to record Application usage. It may open the accessibility settings for you; if you leave, reopening AWARE brings you back there.

Android restricts this setting for apps installed outside the Play Store, so on most phones your first try is blocked with a Restricted setting message. That’s expected: Android only offers Allow restricted settings after you’ve seen that message once.

The screenshots are from a Samsung Galaxy. If your menus look different, find the route for your phone.

Try to turn on AWARE

  1. Go to SettingsAccessibilityInstalled appsAWARE and turn on AWARE. Your phone may call this menu Installed services or Downloaded apps.
  2. If you see a Restricted setting message, tap OK and continue below. If Android asks to allow AWARE full control instead, tap Allow and go to step 4.

Allow restricted settings

  1. Open your phone’s SettingsAppsAWARE.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right, then tap Allow restricted settings. Confirm with your PIN, pattern or fingerprint if asked. On some phones the option is listed directly on the App info page instead of in the menu.

No Allow restricted settings option?

Android hides it until you’ve tried to turn on AWARE and seen the Restricted setting message. Do the first part again, tap OK on the message, then reopen AWARE’s App info.

Turn on AWARE

  1. Go back to SettingsAccessibilityInstalled appsAWARE, turn on AWARE and tap Allow.

Step 4: Join the study

  1. Open AWARE and tap Join a Study.
  2. Copy this URL and paste it into the URL field in AWARE.
    Study URL {{ config_url }}
  3. Leave the password field empty and tap Join.
  4. Read the study description and tap Sign Up!
5

Step 5: Set up study permissions

On the Study permissions screen, review what the study collects. Tap Enable for each sensor that asks for permission.

Location
If prompted, tap Allow in settings, choose Allow all the time, then return to AWARE.
Wi‑Fi, calls and messages
Tap Allow when asked.
Application usage
If it won’t turn on, close AWARE and follow step 3. Reopen AWARE and try again.
6

Step 6: Check and continue

Check that each permission shows Granted. Sensors marked Automatic are collected after you join. Tap Agree & Continue.

That’s the whole setup

Once AWARE shows its list of sensors, you’re done. You can head back to your dashboard.
